# Scientific writing

Use the requested language and the supplied article structure. Synthesize the available evidence into readable paragraphs, keeping the subject, scope, and observation level of each claim clear. Distinguish an observed result from an interpretation, a hypothesis, or an author's proposal. Preserve meaningful qualifications, comparison directions, and uncertainty. Treat missing material as a limit on the claim.
