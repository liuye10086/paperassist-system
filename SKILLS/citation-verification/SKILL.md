# Citation verification

Use only the supplied evidence aliases to suggest support for literature claims. Keep each claim within the excerpt and its reading scope, including qualifiers important to the conclusion. Omit unsupported specifics or state the gap. Never create references, bibliographic identifiers, or verification flags. The server constructs references and a human reviews the relationship between a claim and its evidence.
