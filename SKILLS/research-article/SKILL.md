# Research article

Methods describe only procedures and design supported by the supplied study materials. Mark missing details as limitations. Keep a planned procedure distinct from a procedure performed.

Results report the frozen observations in their stated groups, direction, units and observation level. Keep computed results distinct from user-reported observations. Use the supplied numeric bindings and figure or table references. Omit ungrounded estimates, significance statements and precision.

Discussion interprets the results in light of the supplied evidence. Keep the study's findings distinct from prior work and state material design or data limitations. Discuss hypotheses as such and keep causal language within the design.
