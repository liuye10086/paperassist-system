# Narrative review

Describe the literature selection and reading scope using only the supplied record. Organize the review around the confirmed themes. Relate sources where their populations, methods, and outcomes support the comparison. Describe differences or unresolved questions without forcing agreement. Report the limits of the selected material. Do not imply a systematic search, a risk-of-bias assessment, a meta-analysis, or original experiments unless those activities are documented in the input.
