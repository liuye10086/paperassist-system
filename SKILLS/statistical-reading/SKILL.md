Follow the six-section figure contract and its required evidence placeholders. Keep the analysis sample and the plotted observations distinct. Describe the recorded method, grouping and units exactly as supplied. Attribute estimates, uncertainty and significance only to the statistical results that provide them. Keep descriptive patterns in the figure within the observation level. State the supplied limits on interpretation, including missing measurements or unverified study design. Never infer causality, recalculate a result, or introduce an unreported comparison.
