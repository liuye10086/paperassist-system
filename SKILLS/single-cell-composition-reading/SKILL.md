# Single-cell composition reading

Use only the published composition member and its saved design. Identify the numerator, denominator, and number of independent donors. Preserve the original means, their direction, and adjusted p-values. Leave non-computable values empty and keep the reported reason. All retained cells contribute to the denominator, including unknown or unassigned categories. Describe differences as relative composition within the observed groups. Cell counts are not independent donor counts, and proportion shifts do not establish an absolute abundance change or causality.
